## Main Failure Mode
The baseline can explain the causal mechanism correctly while choosing a different
taxonomy level than benchmark v1, as in CC-005. It also tends to elevate visible but
small/non-material deviations into contributing causes.

## Hot Take / Practical Insight
**More agentic is not automatically better.** The Stage 0 complete-context analyst achieved 90%
RCIA. Tools-only V1 fell to 50%; structured hypothesis discipline recovered to 70%; and V3
adversarial verification produced no RCIA gain while substantially increasing tokens and cost.
The complete-context agentic candidate and adaptive escalation each reached 80%, but neither
outperformed Stage 0. The product decision is therefore to use the simple complete-context
structured analyst by default and retain V2-style deep investigation only as an optional audit or
drill-down capability.

Secondary evaluation lesson: a deterministic metric is only as meaningful as its reference set.
Comparing evidence IDs with a deliberately non-exhaustive allowlist is useful for audit, but calling
every non-allowlisted item an unsupported factual claim overstates what the benchmark proves.
